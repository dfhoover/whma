October Lessons from the Crane

This month's White Crane session with Hanshi Legacy was a much-needed refresher. After a summer hiatus, a bit of rust had settled in, but the session quickly shook that off. We warmed up, delved into the kata up to Hakutsuru Dai, and were tuned up with Hanshi's expert insights. Questions were answered from nuanced movements to intricate bunkai. Kyoshi Dauphin’s demonstration of the next kata left me pumped for the November lesson.

I have been privileged to train Matsumura Hakutsuru White Crane with Hanshi Legacy for several years. My logbook, throughout this journey, has expanded considerably, detailing my (still limited) insights into: the system, kata, and bunkai. This session added a few details. Initially, I intended to include several of my journal additions below, but frankly, they are all written so cryptically, that even I will not understand them without regular review. Instead, I will include only the following note with it's attached video:

  • - Hakutsuru Dai Bunaki eastward at end of kata: the block strike is followed up with a wrist grab & reach behind opponent’s neck for lock.

    • With many of my written description, I now also record short videos. As you can see here, they are informal, quick and simple. (A special thank-you to my wife for humouring me with this, and many, many other lesson videos I’ve recorded over the years.)

    We ended the October session with a striking drill - practicing a very relaxed strike. Working in partners, we threw these as slaps, recognizing that as soon as the technique is in progress, it opens you up to a counter which you must defend. Then building on that to throw a very fast reverse punch, relaxed, with maximum speed, from the hip, but without the hip rotation for maximum speed and to minimize the subsequent target.

    As is always the case, a written description of a class pales in comparison to physically being there. If you have the opportunity to train with Hanshi Legacy, I simply cannot recommend him enough!
